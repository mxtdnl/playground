Your brief

Fix the floor without touching the price list

You are a consultant brought in to a 21-person satellite sales office. Revenue has averaged £200k a month for six months. SDRs leave at two or three a month, a few stars carry everyone else, and the four teams barely talk to each other. You have twelve months.

You can change

  • How people share knowledge and learn
  • How managers manage, coach and communicate
  • Culture, mood, recognition and the office itself
  • Hours, wellbeing and workload
  • How you recruit, onboard and replace leavers

You cannot change

  • Pricing, discounts or the product
  • Pay, commission or bonuses
  • Targets, strategy or marketing
  • Headcount: you can only replace leavers

How a month works

  1. If something happens on the floor, decide how to respond.
  2. Write your plan in plain English. There is no menu of options: you have to come up with the ideas.
  3. Check how your plan was understood, then run the month and read what happened.
